# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Aspirants preparing for competitive Indian Banking examinations (IBPS PO/Clerk, SBI PO/Clerk, IBPS RRB Officer/Clerk, RBI Grade B).

## Product Purpose

PrepTrack is an offline-capable, high-focus exam preparation suite and progress tracker designed to guide aspirants from syllabus onboarding to exam readiness through structured daily goals, spaced repetition quizzes, official exam mock tests (+1 / -0.25 negative marking), and an intelligent question pattern generator.

## Positioning

Unlike generic quiz portals or costly test-series platforms, PrepTrack combines offline algorithmic pattern generation with frugal on-demand AI and spaced repetition. It prevents question duplication, tracks mastery per topic, works seamlessly offline via PWA, and stores everything locally with complete user privacy.

## Operating Context

- Rigorous daily study blocks (Pomodoro timer, subject breakdown).
- High-speed arithmetic and reasoning topic drills (45s speed timers).
- Full 60-minute sectional mock exam simulations with standard IBPS marking schemas.
- Progress visualization and admin telemetry for question bank health.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offline-first PWA with IndexedDB and local cache storage.
- Multi-topic selection and balanced question generation across Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English Language, General Awareness, and Computer Knowledge.
- Configurable dual Gemini AI keys with automated fallback and algorithmic offline generator.
- No forced external cloud accounts required for primary functionality.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: PrepTrack (Bank Exam Suite).
- Personality: Crisp, authoritative, distraction-free, encouraging, dependable.
- Palette: Trustworthy banking navy, sharp cobalt blue, crisp slate typography, forest green success, warm amber caution.
- Banished Anti-patterns: Generic AI purple gradients, washed-out gray on dark backgrounds, emoji as pseudo-icons, decorative halos.

## Product Principles

1. **High Information Density Without Clutter**: Aspirants need to scan topics, timers, and question options instantly without visual noise.
2. **True Contrast & Legibility**: Strict contrast ratios, crisp typography, and clear visual weights for intense reading sessions.
3. **Intentional Feedback**: Clear states for selected, correct, reviewed, and mastered items.
4. **Resilient Offline First**: Zero latency for cached content; clear indicators when offline.
